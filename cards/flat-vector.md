# Flat Vector

Target: gallery/flat-vector.jpg

## Look
- No outlines, no gradients, no alpha fades. Things shrink out rather than fade.
- Night palette: navy bands `#132c52`, `#17345f`, `#1c3d6e`, cream `#f3dfc1`, yellow windows `#ffd56b`, teal `#6fd6c5`, red `#e4475b`.
- Big cream moon with craters, two parallax city rows, cream cloud smoke, three-layer teardrop flames, confetti.
- Foreground crowd of plain circles in four skin tones holding yellow phones.

## Keep
`fill()`, `flame()`, city `row()`, parallax with `cam`, crowd block.

## Swap
`rickshaw()`, `man()`, colour of the night for a day version if the topic needs it, caption.

## Motion
Smooth, `sub` 3. The camera rises with the subject, each layer at its own rate.

## Text
Noto 800, white, left aligned at 84 px. Works for long lines, which makes this the safest look for dense scripts. Keep white text off the cream moon: the opening question sits below it, the last line above it once the camera has risen.
